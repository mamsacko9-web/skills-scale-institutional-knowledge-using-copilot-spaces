# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Welcome

This directory contains the complete set of project management processes and guidelines used by OctoAcme to deliver successful projects. Whether you're starting a new initiative, planning a release, or conducting a retrospective, you'll find actionable guidance here.

## OctoAcme Project Management Approach

OctoAcme operates a structured lifecycle approach to project management that prioritizes customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, and data-informed decisions. The organization follows a **customer-first methodology** with five core principles:

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has a named Project Manager and Product Lead
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning

## Process Summary

OctoAcme operates a structured lifecycle approach to project management that spans five major phases:

### 1. **Initiation**
New ideas are validated through a Project One-pager that confirms business need, identifies stakeholders, and establishes success metrics. This lightweight gate ensures alignment before planning begins.

### 2. **Planning**
Once approved, projects move into detailed planning where work is broken into shippable increments, prioritized with acceptance criteria, and mapped against a release timeline. This structured front-end approach reduces rework and scope creep.

### 3. **Execution**
Execution follows an iterative, pull-based delivery model with a consistent team rhythm. The organization uses GitHub Projects boards with standardized columns (Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, Done) and maintains discipline around small pull requests, automated testing, and code review. Quality is woven throughout via unit tests, integration tests, smoke tests, and security scanning in CI.

### 4. **Release**
Features are deployed to production with standardized checklists, smoke tests, and rollback procedures documented in advance. Releases are announced to stakeholders and monitored for issues.

### 5. **Close & Retrospective**
After each sprint, release, or milestone, the team conducts retrospectives to capture learnings and convert them into actionable improvements. Action items are tracked and reviewed in weekly syncs.

## Key Team Roles

OctoAcme defines three primary personas:

- **Developers**: Design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate on implementation, write and maintain tests, participate in reviews, and help identify technical risks.
- **Product Managers**: Define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

## Communication & Risk Management

Communication and risk management are central to OctoAcme's approach:

- **Team rhythm**: Daily standups (15 min), weekly delivery syncs, demos at sprint boundaries
- **Risk management**: Simple Risk Register tracking impact, likelihood, and mitigation; reviewed weekly
- **Escalation path**: Team → PM → Product Lead → Sponsor
- **Stakeholder updates**: Regular status updates using standardized templates highlighting progress, blockers, and decisions needed
