# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Define QA approach (unit, integration, e2e, manual testing)
- Execute acceptance testing and validate features
- Identify quality risks and defects
- Report quality metrics and test coverage
- Participate in release readiness reviews

### Goals
- Ensure features are defect-free and meet acceptance criteria
- Minimize production incidents through thorough testing
- Enable confidence in releases through quality visibility

### Typical Communication
- QA planning during sprint planning and project kickoff
- Defect reports and quality metrics in weekly syncs
- Release readiness reports before deployment

### How They Interact
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on test requirements during sprint planning; provide feedback on defects identified during testing
- **With Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria are testable; ensure quality metrics align with product goals
- **With Project Managers**: Provide testing status updates and release readiness assessments; escalate quality blockers
- **With Technical Leads**: Review test approach for technical feasibility and coverage

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define technical direction, conduct design reviews, identify technical risks, and mentor developers. They bridge product requirements and technical implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers and review technical approaches
- Ensure adherence to coding standards and best practices
- Evaluate technology choices and dependencies
- Contribute to capacity planning and effort estimation

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound solutions that scale and maintain
- Reduce technical debt and security vulnerabilities
- Build team capability and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews and mentoring feedback
- Risk identification in planning and execution phases

### How They Interact
- **With Developers**: Provide technical direction, mentor on best practices; approve designs before implementation
- **With Project Managers**: Flag technical risks and dependencies during planning; contribute to effort estimation
- **With Product Managers**: Advise on technical trade-offs and feasibility of requirements
- **With QA/Testing Leads**: Collaborate on test strategy for complex technical components

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approve investments, remove organizational blockers, and communicate outcomes to leadership. They represent customer and business needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success criteria
- Approve project scope and resource allocation
- Remove organizational and cross-team blockers
- Communicate project outcomes to leadership
- Participate in key decision gates
- Provide feedback on solutions and prototypes

### Goals
- Ensure projects align with business strategy
- Maximize return on investment
- Remove barriers to successful delivery

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff and decision gate meetings
- Monthly stakeholder updates and reviews
- Ad-hoc escalation for blockers

### How They Interact
- **With Project Managers**: Provide business context; remove organizational blockers; approve scope changes
- **With Product Managers**: Define success metrics and business priorities; validate market/customer fit
- **With Development Team**: Review prototypes and solutions; provide business feedback; validate outcomes align with expectations

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Officers ensure features meet security and regulatory requirements. They conduct security reviews, manage incident response, and maintain compliance posture.

### Responsibilities
- Review features for security vulnerabilities and compliance risks
- Conduct security testing and penetration testing when needed
- Lead security incident response and triage
- Maintain compliance documentation and audit trails
- Define security acceptance criteria for features
- Escalate critical security issues to leadership

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and data loss
- Maintain regulatory compliance and audit readiness
- Build security awareness across the team

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews during planning and design phases
- Incident notifications and post-incident retrospectives
- Security scanning reports and vulnerability management

### How They Interact
- **With Technical Leads**: Collaborate on secure architecture and design patterns; define security requirements
- **With Developers**: Review code for security vulnerabilities; provide security best practices guidance
- **With Project Managers**: Identify security risks; escalate critical issues; participate in incident response
- **With QA/Testing Leads**: Coordinate security testing and penetration testing activities

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process blockers, and coach teams on continuous improvement. They protect team focus and velocity.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and escalate process blockers
- Coach the team on agile principles and practices
- Track and improve team velocity and burndown
- Support continuous improvement action items
- Remove impediments to productivity

### Goals
- Maintain consistent team velocity and predictability
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Maximize team productivity and psychological safety

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Impediment escalation and tracking
- Retrospective action item follow-up

### How They Interact
- **With Project Managers**: Support delivery tracking; escalate process blockers; coordinate impediment removal
- **With All Team Members**: Facilitate ceremonies; coach on agile practices; remove process barriers
- **With Product Managers**: Protect team focus; manage scope creep through proper planning processes
- **With Team Members**: Foster psychological safety; encourage continuous improvement mindset
